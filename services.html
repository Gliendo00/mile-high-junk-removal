What we do

Junk Removal Services in Denver & the Front Range

Mile High Junk Removal is a locally owned crew covering everything from a single piece of furniture to a full property cleanout — for homeowners, landlords, businesses, contractors and HOA boards alike. If you would rather load it yourself, we also drop a 15-yard dumpster. Everything below is a service we run ourselves, priced the same transparent way.

For your home

Residential junk removal

Every residential job is priced by how much of our 14' x 7' x 4' trailer your items fill, from a $100 minimum pickup up to $600 for a full load. We do the carrying, and usable goods go to donation or recycling before anything heads to the landfill.

Home Cleanouts

From a single spare room to a full property clear-out — we carry everything out ourselves, no sorting or staging required.
Furniture Removal

Couches, mattresses, dressers and anything too heavy to move alone, carried out from inside the home.
Estate Clean Outs

Full-property clearing for families, realtors and estate managers, handled carefully and on your timeline.
Storage Unit Cleanouts

We clear the unit on-site — including gated facilities — so you can stop paying rent on space you no longer need.
Appliance Disposal

Refrigerators, washers and ovens removed, with the Freon-compliant recycling paperwork handled for you.
Electronics Recycling

TVs, computers and old office electronics kept out of the landfill and recycled in full compliance.
Yard Waste Removal

Branches, leaves, old fencing and storm debris hauled off without you bagging or bundling anything.

For business & property

Commercial & property services

These overlap, so here is the short version: start with Commercial Junk Removal if you run a business and are not sure which fits. Property Management covers rental units and apartment communities, Office Junk Removal covers workspaces and their furniture, Contractor & Remodeler covers active job-site debris, and HOA covers shared community areas. Any of them can be a single visit or a standing arrangement.

Commercial Junk Removal

The starting point for any business job — a one-time clearing or a standing arrangement across several locations.
Property Management

Turnovers between tenants, eviction leftovers and shared-space pickups, scheduled around your leasing calendar.
Office Junk Removal

Desks, chairs, cubicles and old equipment cleared around your business hours rather than during them.
Contractor & Remodeler

Job-site debris cleared mid-project so your crew keeps working instead of making dump runs.
HOA Junk Removal

Bulk items at the dumpster corral, illegal dumping and community cleanup days, coordinated with your board.
Foreclosure & REO Cleanouts

Fast trash-outs on bank-owned properties for agents, asset managers and trustees, timed to a listing date.
Light Demolition

Sheds, decks, fences and hot tubs torn down and hauled away in the same visit.
