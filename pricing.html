Common questions

Pricing FAQ

How is junk removal priced?

By trailer space, not by the hour. Pricing runs from a $100 minimum pickup up to $600 for a full 14' x 7' x 4' trailer, with up to 1 hour of labor included in every job.

What does the $349 dumpster rental include?

The $349 base rate includes delivery, pickup, up to 5 days on site, and up to 2 tons of accepted material.

Are delivery and pickup included?

Yes — delivery and pickup are both included in the $349 base rental price, with no separate delivery or pickup fee added later.

What happens if the dumpster exceeds 2 tons?

Extra weight is billed at $90 per additional ton, prorated to the actual scale weight — we'll always let you know before it becomes a charge.

Are there extra charges for certain items?

Yes. A handful of items — tires, TVs, paint, and Freon-containing appliances — carry their own flat disposal fee for junk removal, listed above. For dumpster rentals, some materials need approval or carry an added fee; see our dumpster rental page for the full list.

Is labor included with junk removal?

Yes — up to 1 hour of labor is included with every junk removal job. Additional labor beyond that is billed at $125/hour.
