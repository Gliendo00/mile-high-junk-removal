A Simple Seasonal Cleanout Guide for Denver Homeowners

August 6, 2026 · 5 min read

No matter how organized you start the year, stuff finds a way to pile up. A garage corner turns into a dumping ground, a spare room fills with boxes "for later," and before long it feels like too much to tackle at once. The good news: most of it follows a pattern by season, so it's easier to stay ahead of than it looks.

Spring

Spring is when winter's accumulation finally gets dealt with — holiday decorations that never made it back to storage, winter gear taking up closet space, and the general "let's finally deal with this" energy that comes with warmer weather. It's also prime time for garage and basement cleanouts, since it's finally comfortable enough to spend a few hours out there sorting through what's actually worth keeping.

Summer

Summer tends to be about outdoor spaces — old patio furniture that didn't survive winter, a deck or fence project that leaves behind a pile of debris, or finally getting rid of that shed's worth of stuff you've been stepping around. It's also a popular season for moving, which means its own wave of furniture and boxes that don't make the cut.

Fall

Fall brings yard waste — branches, leaves, and general cleanup before the first freeze — plus a second wind of decluttering as people start prepping their homes before the holidays roll around. If you're planning to host, this is usually when the "get the house presentable" pile shows up.

Winter

Winter is the quiet one — mostly post-holiday cleanup (boxes, packaging, the stuff that got replaced by new gifts) and whatever New Year's decluttering resolutions actually stick. Loads tend to be smaller this time of year, which works fine with how we price things.

However it piles up, the process stays the same

Whatever season it is when you finally get to it, the process on our end doesn't change — we price by how much of our trailer your stuff fills, not by the hour, so a small spring closet purge costs a lot less than a full garage clear-out. And anything still in good shape gets set aside for donation before it ever heads to the landfill — we regularly work with local donation centers and nonprofits for exactly that reason. Curious exactly how that sorting works? See what happens to your junk after we haul it away.

If you've got a pile building up right now, you don't have to wait for the "right" season to deal with it. Text us a few photos and we'll give you a real estimate before we even show up, or book online any time it works for you.

Common questions

Seasonal cleanout FAQ

Does junk removal cost more depending on the season?

No — pricing is always based on how much space your items take up in our trailer, not the time of year. A small spring closet purge costs the same as a small load any other time of year.

Can I book same-day pickup during a busy season?

Same-day and next-day service is available year-round. Booking a little ahead of time simply makes it easier to lock in your preferred time window.

What happens to items I set aside for donation?

Anything still in good shape is set aside before pickup and kept out of the landfill — see our full breakdown in what happens to your junk.
