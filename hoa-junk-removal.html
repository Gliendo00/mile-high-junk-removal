Who this is for

Built for HOA boards, managers & community associations

HOA boards, community association managers, property managers, condo associations, and townhome communities across Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Littleton, Arvada, and the surrounding Front Range call us when bulk items, illegal dumping, or common-area junk becomes a recurring headache. You get one point of contact instead of juggling a different hauler every time something turns up.

The work

What we handle at your community

Every property is different, but most HOA calls fall into a few categories.

Bulk Item Removal

Couches, mattresses, furniture, appliances, shelving, and boxed junk — abandoned or set out for pickup. Our crew handles the lifting and hauling.

Illegal Dumping Cleanup

Dumpster enclosures, alleys, parking areas, sidewalks, and other shared spaces cleared once items show up. We handle removal, not enforcement.

Common Area Cleanup

Clubhouses, storage rooms, maintenance rooms, shared garages, parking structures, and community offices — plus yard debris where it applies.

Move-Out & Abandoned Items

Belongings left behind in condo and townhome communities. The board or manager confirms items are authorized for removal before we go.

Community cleanup days

Coordinate a community-wide cleanup day

A lot of HOAs run a defined cleanup event — spring cleanup, fall cleanup, an annual bulk-item day, or a push during a heavy move-out period. Residents bring approved items to a designated drop area and we handle the hauling from there.

These events work best when scope, accepted materials, expected volume, and scheduling are set ahead of time — not an unlimited drop-off. Call or text us before the date and we'll work out the details with your board or management team.

Recurring service

One call or a standing arrangement — both work

Some communities call us once for a specific problem. Others set up monthly, seasonal, or as-needed service for bulk items and dumpster-area cleanup that comes up on a predictable basis. There's no contract required either way — call, text, or book online whenever it's needed.

What we can't take

Hazardous chemicals, asbestos, biohazard material, and unknown liquids are never accepted. Let us know ahead of a scheduled pickup if there's any question about a specific item.

Pricing

Priced by volume & scope, not a flat community rate

A standard bulk pickup or dumpster-area cleanup is priced the same transparent way as every job we do — by volume, item type, weight, and access, starting around a $100 minimum pickup and running up to $600 for a full trailer load, with up to 1 hour of labor included.

A community cleanup day, a large bulk-item event, or a property with multiple pickup points usually works better as a custom quote rather than a single per-visit price — send us the scope and we'll put a number together in advance.

The process

How it works for an HOA or community cleanup

1

The HOA board, association manager, or property manager contacts us.

2

Send photos and describe the scope — what needs to go and where it is on the property.

3

We confirm access, pickup locations, and which items are authorized for removal.

4

6

The HOA or management team is responsible for confirming that items are authorized for removal before we haul them away.

A note on abandoned or resident belongings

We regularly clear belongings left behind in managed communities, but we're not positioned to advise on notice requirements or the legal side of abandoned property — that's a call for your board, management company, or attorney. Once you've confirmed items are cleared for removal, we handle the physical hauling. Our rental property cleanout guide covers some of what's involved on the residential side, if useful background.

Common questions

HOA junk removal FAQ

Do you work directly with HOA boards and community managers?

Yes. We coordinate directly with board members, community association managers, and property managers — whoever handles logistics for your community — on scheduling, access, and what's authorized for removal.

Can you remove items left near community dumpsters?

Yes, clearing furniture, mattresses, and bagged junk left at dumpster enclosures and dumpster corrals is one of the most common calls we get from HOAs and property managers.

Do you clean up illegal dumping?

Yes — we handle the removal once items show up in alleys, parking areas, sidewalks, or other shared spaces. We're not an enforcement or security service; we just come haul it away.

Can you remove mattresses and furniture from common areas?

Yes — mattresses, couches, and other bulky furniture from clubhouses, storage rooms, parking structures, and other common areas are all part of the work we do at communities.

Can you help with HOA community cleanup days?

Yes. We work with boards and managers ahead of time to set the date, the drop-off area, what materials are accepted, and expected volume, then handle the hauling once residents bring items out.

Do you offer recurring HOA junk removal?

Yes — monthly, seasonal, or as-needed service for communities that deal with bulk items or dumpster-area cleanup on an ongoing basis. No contract is required to set that up.

Can you service condo and townhome communities?

Yes — condo associations, townhome communities, and managed residential communities of all sizes across the Denver metro.

Can you provide invoices?

Yes, we provide an invoice or receipt after service for your records or for board/management reimbursement.

Do residents have to be present?

No — for common-area and community pickups, we coordinate access directly with the board or property manager, so residents don't need to be there.

Can you remove items from multiple areas of the property?

Yes — a dumpster corral, a storage room, and a clubhouse can all be handled in the same visit if they're on the same property.

What items can you not take?

Hazardous chemicals, asbestos, biohazard material, and unknown liquids are never accepted. Let us know ahead of time if a cleanup might involve anything like that.

How quickly can an HOA cleanup be scheduled?

Often same-day or next-day for a standard bulk pickup or dumpster-area cleanup. Larger community cleanup days need more lead time since they involve coordinating a date, location, and scope in advance.
