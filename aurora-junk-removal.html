What happens to your stuff after pickup

Furniture, housewares, and clothing from Aurora pickups still in usable shape get pulled aside for local donation centers and nonprofits. Metal, cardboard, and other recyclables get separated out along the way. Anything left — genuinely worn out or unusable — goes to the landfill last, only after donation and recycling are both exhausted.

What we can't take

  • Hazardous materials in bulk — paint and similar items are fine in normal household amounts (see pricing for per-can fees), but not drums or commercial quantities
  • Asbestos, biohazards, or anything requiring specialized hazmat handling
  • Firearms and ammunition
