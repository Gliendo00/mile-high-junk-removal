How to Get Rid of an Old Mattress in Denver

November 18, 2026 · 4 min read

A new mattress shows up, and the old one ends up leaning against a garage wall for months. It's big, floppy, hard to fit in a car, and most trash carts won't touch it. Here are your real options around the Denver metro, from free city pickup to having it carried out for you.

Option 1: Denver Large Item Pickup

If your home is on City of Denver trash service, mattresses and box springs are accepted at Large Item Pickup, which runs every nine weeks on your regular trash day. The catch: they have to be bagged and wrapped, set out by 6 a.m. on your pickup day, and they count toward the 5-item limit per address. Setting a mattress out on any other day counts as illegal dumping. Look up your date on the city's site.

Option 2: Your city or hauler's program

Outside Denver, it depends on where you live. Our city-by-city bulky item guide has the full rundown, but for mattresses specifically:

  • Thornton: Click & Haul takes mattresses by appointment, though a per-mattress fee may apply
  • Westminster: the city's Sustainability Center accepts mattresses and box springs for a fee, if you can drive them there
  • Lakewood: the city points mattresses to fee-based recycling
  • Aurora: the city lists mattress recycling options, and whether your hauler takes one depends on your plan
  • Most other suburbs: it's up to your private hauler or HOA, and it may cost extra or need to be scheduled

Option 3: Donate it, if it qualifies

Donation is worth a try for a clean, newer mattress, but don't count on it. Many donation centers turn away mattresses and box springs that are stained or older than their cutoff, and that cutoff varies by organization. Call ahead before you load it up. Our furniture donation guide covers what usually gets accepted.

Don't toss it in a rented dumpster without asking

If you're renting a dumpster for a bigger project, know that mattresses are one of the items that may need prior approval or carry an added disposal fee, because disposal-facility rules control what goes in. Check before you throw one in. Our dumpster rental page lists the other restricted items.

Option 4: Have it hauled away

Hiring a crew makes sense when your pickup date is weeks out, the mattress is upstairs or in a basement, you're getting rid of more than one, or you'd rather not wrestle it into plastic and down to the curb. We carry it out from wherever it is, no bagging needed. Mattresses and box springs are recycled wherever a facility accepts the materials, and anything left over is disposed of last, not first. Common questions

Mattress disposal FAQ

Can I put a mattress out for Denver trash pickup?

Only on your Large Item Pickup day, which comes every nine weeks for homes on City of Denver trash service. The mattress has to be bagged and wrapped and out by 6 a.m. Setting it out on any other day counts as illegal dumping.

Does the mattress have to be bagged?

For Denver Large Item Pickup, yes. Mattresses and box springs must be bagged and wrapped. When we haul it, no bagging is needed. We carry it out as is.

Will a donation center take my old mattress?

Sometimes. Many turn away mattresses that are stained or older than their cutoff, which varies by organization. Call ahead before loading it up.

Can I throw a mattress in a rented dumpster?

Ask first. Mattresses are one of the items that may need prior approval or carry an added disposal fee in a dumpster, because disposal-facility rules control what goes in.

Do you recycle mattresses?

Mattresses and box springs are recycled wherever a facility accepts the materials. Anything left over is disposed of last, not first.
