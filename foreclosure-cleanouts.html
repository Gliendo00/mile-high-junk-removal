Foreclosure & REO Cleanouts

Foreclosure & REO Property Cleanouts in Denver

Who this is for

Built for the real estate side of a foreclosure

  • Listing agents handling a bank-owned (REO) property
  • Bank & lender asset managers
  • Trustees and attorneys handling a foreclosure estate
  • Property preservation companies
  • Investors buying a property at auction or in pre-foreclosure
  • Title companies coordinating a fast closing timeline

If you're a landlord or property manager dealing with a standard tenant move-out or eviction leftover instead, our property management page covers that recurring relationship. If the cleanout is tied to a death in the family rather than a foreclosure, see estate cleanouts.

What's included

A full trash-out, not just a partial pickup

Foreclosed and bank-owned properties are often left with everything still inside — furniture, appliances, personal belongings, trash, sometimes yard debris out back. A trash-out means clearing all of it, inside and out, so the property is empty, broom-swept, and ready for photos or a buyer walkthrough.

Call, text, or book online with the property address and listing timeline, and we'll give you a firm price and the soonest available date — usually same-day or next-day.

Working around your timeline

Turned around before your listing or showing date

Foreclosure and REO timelines usually move fast once a property's ready to list. Tell us the date you need it done by and we'll prioritize accordingly — most trash-outs are same-day or next-day. We can also send before-and-after photos for your file, and an invoice or receipt for reimbursement and closing records.

Who we coordinate with
  • The listing agent directly
  • A bank or lender's asset manager
  • A trustee or attorney handling the estate
  • An out-of-state owner by phone, text, or email
  • A property preservation company on an existing contract

What we can't take

Hazardous materials in bulk, asbestos or suspected asbestos, biohazards, and firearms or ammunition are never accepted. If a property has any of these, let us know ahead of time so we can point you to the right resource.

Common questions

Foreclosure & REO cleanout FAQ

How is this different from your property management service?

Property management cleanouts are usually recurring — tenant turnovers and standing arrangements with a landlord or management company. Foreclosure and REO cleanouts are typically a single trash-out tied to a real estate transaction, coordinated with a listing agent, asset manager, or trustee, and timed to a listing or closing deadline rather than a leasing calendar.

Do you work directly with banks and asset management companies, or only agents?

Both. We coordinate with whoever is handling the property — a listing agent, an asset manager, a trustee, or an attorney — and we can work directly with an out-of-state owner or bank contact by phone, text, or email if no one is local.

How fast can you turn a property around before a listing?

Same-day or next-day in most cases. Tell us the listing or showing date when you reach out and we'll prioritize accordingly.

Can you provide before-and-after photos and an invoice?

Yes — photos for your file or to send the property owner, and an invoice or receipt for reimbursement and closing records.

What if the property is full — furniture, appliances, and personal belongings left behind?

That's the standard scope for a trash-out — we clear everything left in the home, inside and out, and leave it broom-swept and ready for photos or a walkthrough.
