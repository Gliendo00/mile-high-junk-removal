Pricing

How Our Pricing Actually Works

August 4, 2026 · 4 min read

One of the most common questions we get is "how much is this going to cost?" — and it's a fair one, because a lot of junk removal companies make that surprisingly hard to answer up front. Ours isn't complicated: we price by how much of our 14' x 7' x 4' trailer your items fill.

Priced by trailer space, not by the hour

A $100 minimum pickup covers a small load, and pricing scales up in eighths of a trailer from there, topping out at $600 for a completely full load. We're pricing the room your items take up, not their weight, so a trailer's worth of light boxes costs the same as a trailer's worth of heavier furniture. For the full breakdown by load size, see our pricing page.

What's included in every price

Up to 1 hour of labor is included at every price point, so you're not watching a clock while our crew works. Additional labor beyond that first hour is billed at $125/hour, but that only comes up on the bigger jobs. If anything changes once we're on site, we always talk it through with you before doing any additional work, so the final number never comes as a surprise.

Items with their own disposal fee

A handful of items carry their own flat disposal fee on top of the trailer-space price — things like tires, TVs, paint, and Freon-containing appliances, since those require special handling. Those fees are published, not hidden, and we confirm them with you before we start loading.

Why we price it this way

The upside of pricing by trailer space is simple: you know roughly what a job costs before we even show up, based on how much space your stuff takes up — not on how efficiently our crew happens to move that day. Common questions

Pricing FAQ

Do I get a price before you arrive?

Yes — text a few photos to 303-990-1812 and we'll send back a real price based on trailer space before we ever show up.

Does the price change once you're on site?

Only if the load turns out to be noticeably different than described, and we always confirm any change with you before doing additional work — no surprise charges at the end.

Are disposal fees included in the trailer-space price?

No — they're separate, flat fees for specific items like tires, paint, Freon-containing appliances, and TVs, added on top of the trailer-space price. Full amounts are listed on our pricing page.

Is there a charge for extra labor?

Up to 1 hour of labor is included in every price. Additional labor beyond that is billed at $125/hour, which only comes up on larger jobs.
