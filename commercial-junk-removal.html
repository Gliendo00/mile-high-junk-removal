How this works

One-time job or ongoing service — both work

Some businesses and properties need us once: a single office cleanout, a bulk-item pickup at a commercial building, or a load of renovation debris hauled away. Others have us back regularly — recurring apartment turnovers, periodic bulk-item removal at a property, or ongoing cleanups as a project moves along. Whether you need us once or throughout the year, we can handle the job as it comes up — just call, text, or book online whenever you're ready.

A few examples of what that looks like in practice: a one-off office cleanout, recurring apartment turnovers, periodic bulk-item removal, a broader property cleanup, renovation or remodel debris, and dumpster needs for a project that runs longer than a single visit.

Pricing

Priced by the job, not a flat commercial rate

The same transparent, volume-based pricing applies whether the job is residential or commercial: what you pay comes down to how much space your items take up in the trailer, starting around a $100 minimum pickup and topping out near $600 for a full load, with up to an hour of labor built in. Building access, extra labor, or special-item disposal can add to that — we'll always confirm the numbers with you before we start.

For a larger project — a full building cleanout or an ongoing renovation — a 15-yard dumpster rental can be a better fit than a crew pickup.

Common questions

Commercial junk removal FAQ

Do you handle both one-time and ongoing commercial junk removal?

Yes. Some businesses and properties call us for a single job — a vacated office, a bulk pickup, a load of renovation debris — and others have us out on a recurring basis for things like apartment turnovers or periodic bulk-item removal. Whether it's a one-time job or something that comes up throughout the year, we can handle it as it happens.

What types of businesses and properties do you work with?

Offices and other commercial spaces, apartment communities and rental properties, and businesses or properties generating renovation or remodel debris. If you're not sure where your job fits, call or text us and we'll point you to whichever of our services is the right match.

Can you work around our business or property schedule?

Yes. We regularly schedule around business hours, tenant occupancy, and building access requirements, and we coordinate directly with whoever handles logistics on your end — an owner, manager, or on-site staff.

Do you offer dumpster rental for commercial projects?

Yes, a 15-yard dumpster is available for properties and projects that would rather load on their own schedule instead of booking a crew pickup. See our dumpster rental page for pricing and rules.

How is commercial junk removal priced?

The same transparent way as every job we do — by how much space your items take up, not a flat per-job or per-unit rate. A single pickup costs less than a full office or unit clearout. See our pricing page for the full breakdown.

What areas do you serve?

Denver and the surrounding Front Range, including Aurora, Lakewood, Parker, Arvada, Littleton, Westminster, Thornton, Englewood, Broomfield, and Castle Rock.
