15-Yard Dumpster Rental — Rate & Payment Terms

The rental rate is $349.00, which covers delivery, pickup, up to 5 calendar days on site, and up to 2 tons (4,000 lbs) of accepted material. This amount is charged to your payment method today, when your rental is booked.

If the material collected exceeds 2 tons, Mile High Junk Removal may charge $125.00 per additional ton, prorated to the actual scale weight (equivalent to $0.0625 per pound over the included 4,000 lbs) — never rounded up to a full ton, with the final overweight charge rounded only to the nearest cent. If the dumpster is kept on site beyond 5 calendar days, Mile High Junk Removal may charge $15.00 per additional day. Mile High Junk Removal will review any such charge and give you the opportunity to discuss it before it is submitted to your payment method — no additional charge happens automatically.

By checking the box below and completing this booking, you authorize Mile High Junk Removal to charge the payment method you provide today for the $349.00 base rental, and to charge that same payment method for any additional charge described above, after that charge has been reviewed as described.

Placement location, accepted/prohibited materials, and any other site-specific details are confirmed with you directly at delivery. This summary describes the rental rate and payment mechanics only and is not a complete statement of all rental terms; it does not constitute legal advice.
