Choosing the right option

Garage cleanout crew or a dumpster?

If the garage is mostly loose clutter and you want it gone in a single visit without lifting anything yourself, a crew is usually the simpler choice — we do the carrying, loading and sorting for you.

If you’re tackling the garage slowly over a week, or it’s part of a bigger project where debris builds up over several days, our 15-yard dumpster rental lets you load at your own pace. Our dumpster vs. junk removal guide walks through the trade-offs, and if you’d rather plan the sorting yourself first, the garage cleanout guide has a step-by-step approach.

What happens to your stuff after pickup

Furniture, housewares, and clothing from garage cleanouts still in usable shape get pulled aside for local donation centers and nonprofits. Metal, cardboard, and other recyclables get separated out along the way. Anything left — genuinely worn out or unusable — goes to the landfill last, only after donation and recycling are both exhausted.

What we can't take

  • Hazardous materials in bulk — paint and similar items are fine in normal household amounts (see pricing for per-can fees), but not drums or commercial quantities
  • Asbestos, biohazards, or anything requiring specialized hazmat handling
  • Firearms and ammunition
  • Leftover chemicals beyond normal household amounts — pesticides, pool chemicals, solvents or fuel — belong at a household hazardous waste drop-off; ask if you’re not sure

Common questions

Garage cleanout FAQ

Do I need to sort or box things before you arrive?

No. Point out what's staying and we sort the rest as we load — loose items, half-open boxes and piles are all fine. If there's something you haven't decided on yet, set it to one side and we'll work around it.

Can you take an old garage fridge or freezer?

Yes. Refrigerators, freezers and other Freon-containing appliances carry a $25 disposal fee each, on top of the trailer-space price. The full list of special-item fees is on our pricing page.

What about old paint cans and tires?

Paint in normal household amounts is fine at $15 per can. Tires are $5 each without a rim and $10 each with one. Drums or commercial quantities of paint or chemicals are something we can’t take.

Can you remove shelving units and workbenches?

Freestanding shelving and workbenches, yes. If something is bolted to the wall or built in, mention it when you send photos so we can tell you up front whether it's something we can take out.

How much does a garage cleanout cost?

It depends entirely on how much of our trailer the garage fills — from a $100 minimum up to $600 for a full 14' x 7' x 4' load, with up to 1 hour of labor included. Send a few photos for an estimate, and we'll confirm the price on-site before loading begins.

Do you clean out sheds and attics too?

Yes — sheds, attics and crawl-space storage are handled the same way as the garage. If the shed itself needs to come down, that’s a light demolition job we can do in the same visit.
