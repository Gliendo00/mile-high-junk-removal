Construction Debris Removal in the Denver Metro

August 8, 2026 · 6 min read

A renovation project produces a specific kind of mess — drywall dust, torn-up flooring, old cabinetry, scraps that don't fit neatly into a household junk category. Here's what we can take, what needs a different approach, and how it's priced.

What we take

Drywall, flooring (tile, hardwood, carpet), cabinetry, fixtures, trim, and general framing scraps from a renovation or small demo project are all routine for us. If it came out of a wall, floor, or kitchen during a remodel, chances are we can haul it.

What needs a different approach

Dirt, rock, concrete, and brick are all things we take, but they're priced by volume rather than standard trailer space, since they're far heavier per cubic foot than typical debris. Suspected asbestos, lead paint, or other hazardous materials require a licensed abatement contractor rather than standard junk removal — always worth flagging before any demo work starts if the home is older.

Single pickup vs. a standing dumpster

For a single room's worth of debris, one pickup usually covers it. For a project generating debris continuously over several weeks — a full kitchen gut, a multi-room remodel — a dumpster parked on-site can be more practical than scheduling repeat pickups. Our dumpster rental vs. junk removal comparison walks through how to decide which fits your project.

Sorting what's recyclable

Metal fixtures and framing components get pulled out and recycled where possible, rather than treated the same as drywall or flooring debris headed for disposal. It's the same donation-and-recycling-first approach we apply to every home cleanout and job site.

Commercial and office projects

If the debris is coming out of a commercial space rather than a home, our office junk removal service covers renovation debris from that side too, including old furniture and fixtures being replaced.

For broader commercial cleanout needs beyond a single office, our commercial junk removal services cover businesses and properties across Denver.

If you're a contractor or remodeler generating debris across an active job site, our contractor and remodeler junk removal service is built for recurring pickups throughout a project, not just a single cleanout.

What it costs in Broomfield

A single room's worth of renovation debris typically falls in the 1/4 to 1/2 load range, $200 to $350, though larger demo projects run higher based on volume. Text a few photos of what's coming out and we'll send back a real estimate, or book online to schedule your pickup.

Common questions

Construction debris FAQ

What kind of construction debris can you remove?

Drywall, flooring, cabinetry, fixtures, trim, and general framing scraps from a renovation or small demo project are all things we regularly haul.

Do you take dirt, rock, or concrete?

Yes, though it's priced by volume separately from standard trailer-space pricing, since material like this is far heavier per cubic foot than typical household debris.

Can you handle a demo project's debris in one visit?

For a single room or small project, usually yes. For a project generating debris continuously over several weeks, a dumpster rental often ends up more practical — see our dumpster rental comparison for how to decide.

What about hazardous materials like asbestos?

Suspected asbestos, lead paint, or similar hazardous materials need a licensed abatement contractor, not standard junk removal — we're happy to point you in the right direction if that comes up on a job.

Is construction debris priced differently than household junk?

General construction debris like drywall and flooring is priced the same way as household items, by trailer space. Dense materials like dirt, rock, concrete, or brick are priced by volume instead, due to their weight.

How much does construction debris removal cost in Broomfield?

A single room's worth of renovation debris typically falls in the 1/4 to 1/2 load range, $200 to $350, though larger demo projects run higher depending on volume.
