Priced by trailer space

Every job is priced by how much of our 14' x 7' x 4' trailer your items fill — from a $100 minimum pickup up to a $600 full load, with up to 1 hour of labor included. Extra time beyond that hour is billed hourly at $125/hour, but we'll always tell you before it happens — no surprise fees.

The process

How electronics recycling works

1

Tell us what you have — a single laptop or a whole closet of old electronics — for a quick quote.

2

Wipe personal data from any device before we arrive — see our FAQ below for more on that.

3

Our crew boxes and carries everything out — no need to bring it to the curb.

4

Everything goes to a certified electronics recycler, not a landfill.

What happens to your electronics after pickup

Electronics are taken to a certified e-waste recycler where materials are broken down and processed to meet Colorado e-waste regulations. Metals, plastics, and glass are separated and recycled — nothing goes to a landfill as a first option. TVs and monitors go through the same certified process regardless of size.

What we can't take

  • Devices with hazardous leaks — like swollen or leaking batteries — that haven't been isolated first
  • Bulk commercial quantities of toner or ink cartridges — household quantities only

Common questions

Electronics recycling FAQ

Do you take old tube TVs (CRTs), not just flat screens?

Yes, we take CRTs along with flat screens — the size fee tiers apply either way.

What should I do with personal data on my computer before pickup?

We recommend wiping the drive or removing it yourself first. We recycle the hardware responsibly, but we're not a certified data destruction provider.

Is there a size cutoff that changes the price for TVs?

Yes, TVs under 48" and 48" and over are priced differently — see the full breakdown on our pricing page.

Can you recycle small electronics like phones, routers, and cables?

Yes, smaller electronics are usually included with a larger pickup at no extra hassle.

Do you take printers, copiers, and other office electronics?

Yes — for a full office clearout, our office junk removal service covers everything at once.

Where do the electronics actually go after you pick them up?

To a certified e-waste recycler that meets Colorado's e-waste regulations, not a landfill.

Can you pick up just a couple of items, or is there a minimum?

A couple of items is a normal pickup for us, priced at or near our minimum.
