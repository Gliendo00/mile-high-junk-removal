What we handle at a storage facility

  • On-site pickup at any storage facility, including gated properties
  • Access coordinated ahead of time — gate code, unit code, or through the facility office
  • No need to be present once access is arranged
  • Unit left broom-clean once it's cleared
  • Storage units combined with a home or estate cleanout when needed

Priced by trailer space

Storage units are priced the same way as everything else — by how much of our 14' x 7' x 4' trailer your items fill, from a $100 minimum pickup up to a $600 full load, with up to 1 hour of labor included. Extra time beyond that hour is billed hourly at $125/hour, but we'll always tell you before it happens — no surprise fees.

What affects the price: the size of the unit, how full it is, and whether facility access needs extra coordination time.

The process

How a storage unit cleanout works

1

Reach out with the unit size and how full it is — text a few photos or just describe it, and we'll send back a real estimate.

2

Let us know the access details — a gate code, a unit code, or how to coordinate with the facility office.

3

Our crew loads everything from the unit into the trailer and sweeps it out broom-clean when they're done.

4

You cancel the unit or hand back the keys — you don't need to be on-site unless you want to be.

Clearing a unit alongside a home or estate

If the unit is tied to a larger project — clearing a home cleanout alongside it, or working through an inherited estate — we can treat it as one coordinated job or handle each separately, depending on your timing and access. Storage units come up regularly as part of estate cleanouts, and we price and schedule them the same way whether they're attached to a house or standing on their own.

Still deciding what to do with the unit? Our Storage Unit Cleanout Guide covers what to check before you cancel it and how the process typically goes, if you're still in the planning stage.

Common questions

Storage unit cleanout FAQ

Do I need to be at the storage facility for the pickup?

No — as long as access is arranged ahead of time, whether that's a gate code, a unit code, or coordination through the facility office, we can complete the cleanout without you needing to be there.

Can you get into gated or keypad-access storage facilities?

Yes, we regularly clear units at gated facilities — just pass along the access details when you book so our crew can get in.

How is a storage unit priced compared to a house?

The same way — by how much of our trailer your items fill, from a $100 minimum pickup up to $600 for a full load, with up to 1 hour of labor included.

Will the unit be left broom-clean for the facility?

Yes — most facilities expect the unit swept out before you close the account or turn in the keys, and we handle that as part of the job.

Can you combine a storage unit with a home or estate cleanout?

Yes — if the unit is tied to a larger project, we can treat it as one coordinated job or handle each separately, depending on your timeline and access.

How do I get a quote for my unit?

Text a few photos, or just describe the unit size and how full it is, and we'll send back a real estimate — or book online directly to reserve your pickup.
